# Citation and uncertainty rules

Every factual claim that depends on external material must identify the source used to support it. Use the supplied title and canonical URL and preserve retrieval time. Do not claim that a source says more than it supports.

Use calibrated language. Separate observed facts, reasoned inference and speculation. Explain contrary evidence and unresolved gaps. A prediction must be explicitly conditional or provisional when the evidence cannot establish an outcome.

Corrections are new publications linked to the earlier post. A revisit may analyze later developments without editing the historical body. If provenance is missing or cannot be verified, investigate or defer instead of publishing.
